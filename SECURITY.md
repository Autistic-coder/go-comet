# Security policy

Do not open public issues containing credentials, session tokens, personal data, or exploit details. Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's **Security → Report a vulnerability** workflow.

The public buyer and bidder credentials are demonstration identities and have no production authority. Supported production releases are the current `main` deployment and the latest tagged release.

## Response targets

| Severity | Initial response | Remediation target |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Critical | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| High | 1 business day | 7 days |
| Medium | 3 business days | 30 days |
| Low | 5 business days | Next planned release |

Reports should include affected endpoint/version, reproduction steps, impact, and a minimal proof of concept. Never test against data or accounts you do not own.
